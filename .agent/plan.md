# FoodRescue Project Brief

FoodRescue is a platform designed to reduce food waste by connecting local businesses and individuals with excess food to people or organizations in need. This MVP focuses on the core functionality of listing and claiming food items within a local community.

## Features
- **Browse Food Listings**: A real-time list and map view of available food donations in the user's vicinity.
- **Claim Food Item**: A simple workflow for users to reserve a food item for pickup.
- **Post Food Donation**: A streamlined form for donors to quickly upload photos and details of surplus food.
- **User Dashboard**: A personal space to manage active claims, view donation history, and update profile settings.

## High-Level Technical Stack
- **Language**: Kotlin
- **UI Framework**: Jetpack Compose
- **Navigation**: Jetpack Navigation 3 (State-driven navigation)
- **Adaptive Strategy**: Compose Material Adaptive (For optimized layouts across phones, tablets, and foldables)
- **Asynchrony**: Kotlin Coroutines
- **Architecture**: Clean Architecture with MVVM patterns
